> **Not legal advice; review by counsel before use.** Delaware allows either an electronic notice each day the employee
> uses employer email or internet, or a one-time notice the employee acknowledges in writing or electronically.
> "DE" here means the US state of Delaware, not Germany.

# Notice of monitoring

{{employer_name}} monitors employee use of company email, internet and computers with Offload.

Chosen form of notice: {{notice_mode}} (either "daily banner in the Offload Mac app" or "one-time acknowledgment").

## Daily banner text
"Your use of this computer, email and internet is monitored by {{employer_name}}. Offload keeps its detailed activity
record on this Mac for 7 days; the company sees automation counts and daily totals only."

## What the company receives
Automation counts, review times, daily totals and workflows you choose to share. Never message contents, recipients,
web addresses or screenshots.
