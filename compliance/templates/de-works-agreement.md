> **Not legal advice; review by counsel before use.** Software objectively capable of monitoring behaviour or
> performance needs the works council's agreement in Germany, even for a pilot. Without it, sensitive features in
> Offload stay off for DE; with it, only the views listed under "Allowed views" may be enabled.

# Works agreement on the use of Offload

Between {{employer}} and {{works_council}}, effective {{effective_date}}.

## 1. Scope
{{scope}}

## 2. Purpose
Offload finds repeated work and helps automate it. Its outputs are for process improvement only. They may not be used
for performance or behaviour control of individual employees, nor as a basis for employment decisions.

## 3. What is processed
- On the Mac only (deleted after 7 days): activity used to find repeated work.
- In the control plane: automation counts, review times, daily totals, shared workflows.

## 4. Allowed views
{{allowed_views}}

Only views listed here may be enabled in Offload for DE. The list is entered in Offload's enablement record.
Team totals require at least 5 people. Rankings of employees are not allowed.

## 6. Rights of employees and the works council
Employees see everything held about them and who looked at it. The works council may inspect the audit log on request.
