# Written policy on electronic monitoring of employees

**Employer:** {{employer_name}}
**Date this policy was prepared:** {{date_prepared}}
**Dates this policy was changed, and what changed:** {{dates_changed}}

## Do we monitor employees electronically?
Yes. We use Offload, a tool on your work Mac that notices repeated work and helps automate it.

## How we monitor
{{how}}

What Offload does, in plain words:
- On your Mac it records which apps and windows are active and short descriptions of what is on screen. This raw record
  stays on your Mac, is kept for 7 days and is then deleted. Nobody at the company can see it.
- Only these leave your Mac: counts and times of automations you ran, how long you spent reviewing them, totals per day,
  and workflow definitions you chose to share. Never titles, web addresses, message contents, recipients or screenshots.
- You can pause Offload at any time from the Mac menu bar.

## For what purposes we may use the information
{{purposes}}

Offload's numbers are for improving how work gets done. We will not use them as the only reason for any decision about
your pay, discipline or dismissal.

## Who sees what
- Team and department totals are shown only for groups of 5 or more people.
- Per-person views and working-time views are off unless the company turns them on for your province, after a privacy
  assessment and with advance notice to you. If that happens you will be told before it starts.
- You can see everything the company holds about you, and who looked at your data, in Offload's "My data" page.

## Copies and record keeping
We give a copy of this policy to every employee within 30 days of preparing or changing it, and to new employees within
30 days of their start. We keep each version for 3 years after it stops being in effect.

This policy does not create new privacy rights and does not change any rights you already have.
