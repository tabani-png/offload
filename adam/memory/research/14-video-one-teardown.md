# One (getone.one) demo video, frame teardown

Source: ~/Downloads/kyzo_-_meet_One_...mp4 (31.5 s, 1 frame per 2 s read). Audio is AAC; no transcript tool installed; no voice-over text seen in frames.

## Flow, scene by scene
1. 0-2 s: macro photo of an agate stone. "Claude Code" wordmark in white inside four soft pill-shaped corner brackets (a focus frame).
2. 2-4 s: empty warm off-white canvas. Four blue dots mark a frame.
3. 4-6 s: faint square grid. Cards with terminal lines (`you@mac:~/web$ claude`, `you@vps:~$ codex`) and agent chips (CLAUDE CODE, CODEX, OPENCODE) in small-caps mono.
4. 6-8 s: headline "Every agent. One inbox." Dark grey, tight tracking. A dark pill rail peeks in at the right edge.
5. 8-14 s: "Answer in a keystroke." Dark card (claude-7, "Waiting for your answer · web"). User bubble blue, agent bubble grey. Question with 3 numbered options, one marked (Recommended). Input row: camera, S, "Type your answer...", space, mic, V, send. Keycaps (J, K, ?, esc, E) on every action. A large 3D keycap (K, then Return) appears when pressed. The card tilts in 3D on switch.
6. 14 s: success state: green check "push to prod", "esc to undo", thin blue progress bar.
7. 14-16 s: "Press to talk. One routes the message to the right agent." Black pill input. Mic in the rail turns red with a waveform.
8. 16-20 s: "See every agent. On your Mac and your VPS." Dark list titled "one": status dot (blue spinner working, green done, amber waiting), app icon, task name, mic, close. Group header "api · on remote vps". A blue arc sweeps as rows turn green.
9. 20-22 s: "Something's still waiting. And you're away from your Mac." iPhone lock-screen notification "An agent needs your answer."
10. 24-26 s: empty state: "Inbox · Nothing needs you. Suspiciously quiet. Questions land here." White cloud mascot with two eyes.
11. 26-31 s: sunflower field photo, film grain. Mascot + wordmark. "Never keep an agent waiting."

## Persistent UI: right-edge rail
Vertical stack of dark capsules pinned to the screen edge: inbox button (yellow badge when something waits), a capsule with one dot per agent, a capsule with mascot / mic / camera, a "..." button. Always on top, small, quiet.

## Style
- Marketing canvas warm off-white with soft vignette; app surfaces near-black cards, radius ~16-20 px.
- Type: grotesk, large bold headlines, tight negative tracking. UI text 13-15 px, grey secondary. Mono small caps for system labels.
- Color: almost none. Blue accent for user bubble, dots, progress. Green done, amber waiting, blue working, red only while recording.
- Motion: 3D tilt on card switch; keycaps pop when pressed; dots and arcs sweep; eased, not bouncy. One idea per 2 s scene.
- Keyboard first: J/K move, 1-3 pick, E discard, esc undo, space type, V voice, S screenshot.
- Copy: short, dry, a bit funny.

## What we take (principles only, no brand assets)
- Edge rail + inbox as the home surface; our suggestions land there like One's questions.
- Suggestion card = question card: insight, numbered options, (Recommended), keycaps, Discard, esc to undo.
